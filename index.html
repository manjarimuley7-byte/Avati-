Important Safety Information

AVATI is an educational student prototype, not a clinically validated medical device. Sound changes do not prove pain. Pain can occur without vocalization. A clinician must assess the individual. Never use this application instead of emergency medical care. Obtain appropriate consent and ethics approval before testing with patients.
